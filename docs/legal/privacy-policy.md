## 1. What we collect

That is the whole list.

**We set no cookies.** We write nothing to `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or IndexedDB. We
run no analytics, no advertising and no session-replay script, and the site loads no
third-party script of any kind — the fonts are served from our own domain.

**At the date of this draft the form sends nothing anywhere.** It validates what you type and
shows a confirmation, and the address never leaves your browser. This section must be updated
the moment that changes.

| Purpose | Lawful basis |

| Sending you the one launch email you asked for | consent — GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) |

Consent is the only basis we rely on. You give it by ticking a box that is not ticked for you,
and you can withdraw it at any time — every message we send carries a one-click unsubscribe,
and withdrawing does not affect anything we did before you withdrew.

## 4. Who we share it with

Email delivery is handled by a processor under written terms that forbid using your address for

## 5. Where we send it

Our email processor is established in the United States, so sending your address to it is a
transfer outside the EEA and outside Israel. That transfer is covered by Standard Contractual
Clauses together with an assessment of the destination.

restrict processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent you previously gave.
You may also complain to a supervisory authority — in the EU, the authority in your member
state; in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority.

To exercise any of these, email [INSERT_CONTACT_EMAIL]. We answer within 30 days and will tell
you if we need longer. We will not treat you differently for asking.
